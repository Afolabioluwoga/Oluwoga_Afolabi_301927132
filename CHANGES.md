## Kept as they are (explain in the defence)
- The capacity trigger does NOT stop the race under READ COMMITTED; the P3 demo proves it (9/8). The lock or SERIALIZABLE isolation is what fixes it.
- Q2, Q5 and Q7 stay on sequential scans: they read most of their tables, so a Seq Scan with a Hash Join is the correct plan.
- Bulk load disables the two triggers while loading, then re-enables them. Generated data respects the capacity and enrolment rules by construction.
- Ages of generated students are 8 to 15 (a children's chess academy), each with a guardian.
